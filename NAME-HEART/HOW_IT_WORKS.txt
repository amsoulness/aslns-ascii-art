HOW IT WORKS - A Heart for You
ASLNS / @ft.aslns

A glowing heart beats around a name you can change yourself.
scene(t) builds a small colored raster from parametric geometry.
Canvas.draw(t) samples the scene into a grid of 135 columns and 80 rows.
The brightest color channel picks a glyph from a brightness ramp.
Each glyph is tinted with the scene color; a blurred glow is added.
main() displays the artwork, handles controls and loops every 18 seconds.

Click the name box, type up to 24 characters, then press Enter. While editing, R, S, and Space type normally; Esc leaves the field. On mobile, type a name below the player. Share artwork preserves it in the link. Names are not saved into the repository.

Regenerate captures with tools/capture_previews.py NAME-HEART.
Export phone-compatible video with tools/export_mobile.py.
