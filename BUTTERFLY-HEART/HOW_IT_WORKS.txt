HOW IT WORKS - Butterfly Heart
ASLNS / @ft.aslns

Two butterflies fly along a sparkling heart-shaped trail.
scene(t) builds a small colored raster from parametric geometry.
Canvas.draw(t) samples the scene into a grid of 135 columns and 80 rows.
The brightest color channel picks a glyph from a brightness ramp.
Each glyph is tinted with the scene color; a blurred glow is added.
main() displays the artwork, handles controls and loops every 18 seconds.

Regenerate captures with tools/capture_previews.py BUTTERFLY-HEART.
Export phone-compatible video with tools/export_mobile.py.
