HOW IT WORKS - Daisy
ASLNS · @ft.aslns

1. A curved 3D stem, two leaves and five sepals form the plant.
2. Twenty-four radial petals have narrow rounded profiles and fold toward the viewer before opening.
3. A golden-angle spiral distributes 2,400 seed points across the domed yellow center.
4. A reveal wave grows the plant from the ground up. The petals then spread in staggered groups.
5. Lighting, gentle sway, dewdrops, falling droplets, sparks and dust animate the scene.
6. Depth sorting maps 3D samples onto an ASCII grid. Outlines, colored glyphs, trails and soft glow complete the frame.

Run daisy.py after installing requirements.txt.
Space pause, R restart, S screenshot, Esc quit.
Adjust DAISY_PETALS, bloom(), DRAW or COLORS to customize the flower.
